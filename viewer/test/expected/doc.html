struct Georeference

A road plan's georeference - its coordinate system's place on the Earth. The plan's coordinates are a transverse Mercator map projection, with a scale factor of 1, centered on a latitude and longitude - so "x" is the distance east and "y" is the distance north, in the plan's units.
